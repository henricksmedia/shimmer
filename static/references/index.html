Reference library

Play music through this machine and Shimmer measures its tone, then throws the audio away and keeps only the curve. That curve is what the tone target is built from. Developer tooling — not part of the app users see.

Turn off your player's volume normalisation first. Level itself does not matter — the curve is normalised, so a track played 12 dB louder measures identically. But a normaliser also limits when it raises a quiet track, and that changes the audio. Loudness is never recorded from a capture for the same reason. Needs about 20 s of music per track.

green — the median of what you have captured. grey band — where the middle 68 % of it sits. amber — the tone target Shimmer ships today. Where green sits above amber, real music has more energy than the target expects.
