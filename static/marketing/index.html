What to say about Shimmer

This is the page to open before you write anything. The wording below is ready to lift, and every fact on it is checked against the app. If a claim is not on this page, do not make it.

Ten posts, ready to send The plan

1The one line

Use the shortest one that fits the space. They all say the same thing, so you can mix them without contradicting yourself.

Six words

Clean AI music artifacts. Then master.

For a logo lockup, a tab title, or the top of a video.

One sentence

Shimmer takes the fizz out of your AI tracks, then masters them for release. Free, and it runs on your own computer.

The default. Use this one unless you have a reason not to.

A short paragraph

AI music generators leave a thin, fizzy hiss on your track. Normal mastering tools cannot tell it from your music, so they make it louder. Shimmer finds that noise and removes it first, then masters the cleaned track for release. It is free, it runs on your own computer, and it plays back exactly what it removed so you can check its work.

For a store page, a video description, or the first paragraph of an article.

2Who it is for

Three groups, in the order they matter. Write for the first one unless you are somewhere the others gather.

People making AI music

They use Suno, Udio and the rest. They can hear that something is wrong with the top end but cannot name it. They have no audio training and do not want any.

Say: what the noise is, and that they can hear it removed.

Producers who touch AI parts

They know what LUFS and true peak mean. They are checking whether you do before they trust the tool with a track.

Say: one gain move, not multiband. Real numbers. A twelve band EQ they can drive themselves.

People who will not upload

They will not put unreleased music on someone else's server, or they are tired of paying by the month for credits.

Say: free, offline, no account, open source, and free for music you sell.

5How it should sound

Words that work

  • Fizz, hiss, sizzle. Say what people hear.
  • Clean, then master. The order is the point.
  • Free. On your computer. Two facts, four words.
  • Listen to it. Invite the check, do not ask for trust.
  • Real terms. LUFS, true peak and dBTP are fine. Say what they mean the first time.

Words to skip

  • AI powered. The cleanup uses no machine learning at all.
  • Studio quality, pro sound. Everyone says it. It means nothing.
  • Effortless, seamless, unleash, elevate. Empty words.
  • Magic, one click fix. It sets you up to disappoint.
  • Signal chain as a slogan. It is the name of a tab, not a selling line.

6Claims to never make

This is the part that keeps you honest, and out of trouble. Each one below is a thing that sounds good and is not true.

  • "AI powered cleanup." The cleaning path is classic signal processing with no machine learning. That is a strength: the same settings always give the same result. Only the Remix tab uses trained models, and they are other people's, named in the app.
  • "Removes every artifact." It removes a lot, and it shows you what it took. Some damage is baked into the generated audio and cannot be lifted out.
  • "It will get you on playlists." Never promise an outcome you do not control.
  • "Better than paid mastering." Do not rank yourself against services you have not measured. Say what Shimmer does instead.
  • "Use it for anything, no strings." Your music has no strings. The software does: change it or host it as a service and you must publish your source under the same licence.
  • "Works the same on every computer." Stem splitting is much faster with an Nvidia graphics card, and Windows is the most tested system.

7The proof to show, in order

When you have someone's attention for thirty seconds, spend it in this order.

  1. The noise, alone. Play the Removed feed. It is hiss and sizzle, and nothing else.
  2. The same track, before and after. Levels matched, so they judge the sound and not the volume.
  3. The verdict card. One line that says the file is ready to upload.
  4. The price. Free, on your own machine. Say it last, so it lands as a surprise instead of a warning.

Keep this page true

These pages are part of the app folder on purpose. When you change how Shimmer works, change the numbers here in the same sitting. A marketing page that drifts from the product is worse than no page at all.
