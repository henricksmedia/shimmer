What to say about Shimmer

This is the page to open before you write anything. The wording below is ready to lift, and every fact on it is checked against the app. If a claim is not on this page, do not make it.

Posts, ready to send The launch plan

1The one line

Use the shortest one that fits the space. They all say the same thing, so you can mix them without contradicting yourself.

Six words

Clean your AI track. Then master.

For a logo lockup, a tab title, or the top of a video.

One sentence

Shimmer cleans the fizz, the harsh "s" sounds and the mud out of your AI tracks, then masters them for release. Free, and it runs on your own computer.

The default. Use this one unless you have a reason not to.

A short paragraph

AI music generators leave problems in the audio: a fizzy hiss up top, harsh "s" sounds, muddy low mids, a whistle that never changes. Shimmer asks what you hear and turns on the fix made for each one. You can listen to exactly what each fix took out, and what you preview is what you export. Then it masters the track for release, as loud as most released songs by default. It is free, and it runs on your own computer.

For a store page, a video description, or the first paragraph of an article.

2The 2.0 story

A release is a reason to talk. This is what changed, said the way a listener would say it. Lead with the first two.

Tell it what you hear

The 19 presets are gone. You pick cards named for what you hear, like "Harsh, spitty s and sh", and each one turns on the tool made for it: a de-esser, a dynamic EQ, a notch filter, a spectral de-noise. One Amount slider each.

What you preview is what you export

The preview and the export are now the same render. The preview just plays a short part of it. A test fails if the two ever drift apart.

As loud as released songs

The default target is now Commercial, −9 LUFS. In 1.x it was −14, and masters sounded quiet next to released songs. The quieter targets are still one click away.

Every fix was measured first

Each fix was tested on five real songs: how much of its problem it removes, and how much it takes from a song with nothing wrong. The top of each Amount slider comes from that test.

3Who it is for

Three groups, in the order they matter. Write for the first one unless you are somewhere the others gather.

People making AI music

They use Suno, Udio and the rest. They can hear that something is wrong but cannot name it. They have no audio training and do not want any.

Say: pick what you hear, then listen to what it took out.

Producers who touch AI parts

They know what LUFS and true peak mean. They are checking whether you do before they trust the tool with a track.

Say: the preview is the export, each fix is capped by measurement, one gain move and a true-peak limiter, real numbers.

People who will not upload

They will not put unreleased music on someone else's server, or they are tired of paying by the month for credits.

Say: free, offline, no account, open source, and free for music you sell.

6How it should sound

Words that work

  • Fizz, hiss, harsh "s", mud, whistle. Say what people hear.
  • Tell it what you hear. The 2.0 idea in five words.
  • Listen to what it took. Invite the check, do not ask for trust.
  • Turn it down. Talk about the Amount as something you control.
  • Free. On your computer. Two facts, four words.
  • Real terms. LUFS, true peak, de-esser and dynamic EQ are fine. Say what they mean the first time.

Words to skip

  • AI powered. The cleanup is signal processing, plus one small trained model for Shimmer that runs on your computer.
  • Studio quality, pro sound. Everyone says it. It means nothing.
  • Effortless, seamless, unleash, elevate. Empty words.
  • Magic, one click fix. It sets you up to disappoint.
  • Signal chain as a slogan. It is the name of a tab, not a selling line.

7Claims to never make

This is the part that keeps you honest, and out of trouble. Each one below sounds good and is not true.

  • "AI powered cleanup." The cleaning path is classic signal processing, plus one small trained model for the Shimmer card that runs on your computer, with nothing sent anywhere. The same settings always give the same result. The Remix tab also uses trained models, and they are other people's, named in the app.
  • "Gets rid of the Suno shimmer." The Shimmer card turns the fizz down, and it is the newest fix: its listening tests are still to come. Say "turns down", and ask people to listen for themselves.
  • "Fixes clicks and crackle." Not in 2.0. The card says "Not built yet" and changes nothing. The same goes for Phasiness.
  • "Removes every artifact." It removes a lot, and it shows you what it took. Some damage is baked into the generated audio and cannot be lifted out.
  • "It will get you on playlists." Never promise an outcome you do not control.
  • "Better than paid mastering." Do not rank yourself against services you have not measured. Say what Shimmer does instead.
  • "Use it for anything, no strings." Your music has no strings. The software does: change it or host it as a service and you must publish your source under the same licence.
  • "Works the same on every computer." Stem splitting is much faster with an Nvidia graphics card, and Windows is the most tested system.

8The proof to show, in order

When you have someone's attention for thirty seconds, spend it in this order.

  1. The card. Click "Harsh, spitty s and sh" or "Fizzy, flickering hiss up top". People see their own words on screen.
  2. The noise, alone. Press 3. The Removed track is hiss and grit, and nothing else.
  3. The same track, before and after. Levels matched, so they judge the sound and not the volume. Say that you matched them.
  4. The verdict. The release check saying the file is ready to upload.
  5. The price. Free, on your own machine. Say it last, so it lands as a surprise instead of a warning.

Keep this page true

These pages are part of the app folder on purpose, and tests check their numbers against the app. When you change how Shimmer works, change the numbers here in the same sitting. A marketing page that drifts from the product is worse than no page at all.
