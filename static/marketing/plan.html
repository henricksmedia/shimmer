How to get Shimmer 2.0 in front of people

A plan for one person with a day job and no budget. Launch week asks for about an hour a day. After that, about two hours a week. Everything in it is something you can do alone, for free.

1The short version

If you only read one paragraph, read this one.

People making AI music can hear that something is wrong with their tracks, but they cannot name it and they do not know it can be fixed. Version 2.0 meets them where they are: they pick what they hear, in their own words, and they can listen to exactly what was taken out. Show that in a short clip, in the places those people already gather. Answer every comment, and let the free price and the local install close the deal.

The release gives you one loud week. After that you are not running a campaign. You are becoming the person who explains how to finish an AI track. The app follows from that.

2What 2.0 offers

Not a mastering tool. There are hundreds of those. Shimmer treats the problems AI generation leaves in the audio as their own job, fixes them before mastering, and lets you hear the fix.

Four things make 2.0 hard to copy:

  • It asks what you hear. Ten cards named for sounds, each turning on one tool built for it. No presets to decode.
  • It plays back what it removed. Nobody else hands you the proof and invites you to check.
  • The preview is the export. What sounds right in the loop is what ends up in the file.
  • It runs on your machine and costs nothing. Most rivals need an upload, an account and a card.

Everything else, the loudness targets, the EQ, the stems, album mode, is depth for people who stay. Lead with the cards and the Removed track.

4What people use now

Know what you are next to, and be fair about it. Never name a rival to insult it. Describe what Shimmer does and let people work out the rest.

What they useWhy they use itWhere Shimmer differs
Online mastering services Upload a file, get a louder file back, no learning needed. They master the noise along with the music. Shimmer cleans it first, lets you hear what went, and never sees your file.
Repair and mastering plugins Powerful, trusted, and they do far more than Shimmer does. They cost real money and expect you to know what to reach for. Shimmer is free and asks what you hear.
Nothing at all Most people upload the raw generated file. This is your real competition. They do not know the problems have names. Teach that first.

5The one idea to plant

Say this until you are sick of it

Tell it what you hear. Listen to what it took. What you preview is what you export.

Everything you post should push that idea forward. If a post does not, it can still be fun, but it is not doing the job.

6What works on social media

The rules of thumb that hold on every feed. None of them need a budget.

Win the first line

People decide in the first line of a post or the first two seconds of a video. Open with a sound they hate, a surprise, or a question. Never open with "I am excited to announce". The posts page has a bank of first lines.

One idea per post

One problem, one fix, one ask. A post that lists every feature gets scrolled past. Save the list for the README.

Show, then tell

A 20-second clip of the Removed track beats any paragraph. Upload video to each platform directly rather than posting a link to it; feeds show native video to more people.

Sound on, and captions too

Your proof is audio, so say "sound on" in the first second. Most people scroll muted, so put the point in big on-screen text as well.

Links in the comments

Most feeds show posts with links to fewer people. Put the release link in the first comment or your bio, and say "link in the comments".

The first hour matters

Post when you can stay for an hour and answer every comment. Replies tell the feed the post is worth showing, and they are where people decide to try it.

End with one question

"What is the worst sound in your AI tracks?" gets answers. "Check it out!" gets nothing. One ask per post, never three.

A few hashtags, not a wall

Two to four that people actually search, such as #SunoAI, #AImusic and #mastering. On Reddit and LinkedIn, skip them or use very few.

One clip, five posts

Record once. Cut it vertical for short video, keep a still for a screenshot post, pull the words into a text post, and use the full take on YouTube. The same proof, everywhere, in each feed's shape.

9The three weeks after

Week 2 — teach the idea

  • Post Name the fizz (post 14) with no link. Let people ask what helps.
  • Record script B and post it with post 05 as the caption.

Week 3 — earn the skeptics

  • Post Every fix had to earn its place (post 09) where producers read. Expect hard questions and answer them straight.
  • Post What you preview is what you export (post 07) as a short post.

Week 4 — go deeper

  • Record script C and post it with post 08.
  • Post A whole album, kept in shape (post 12) or Split the song (post 13), whichever your people asked about more.
  • Count what happened. See section 12.

10The rhythm after that

Once a month, in this order. It should take one evening.

  1. Ship something. A fix from the changelog's Known issues is the best kind: it shows you listen. There must be a reason to talk.
  2. Write what changed in plain words, the way the changelog does.
  3. Post it in the one or two places that answered last time.
  4. Answer everything for two days.
  5. Release a track as The Treq when you have one, and say what cleaned it.

Do not post on a schedule when you have nothing to say. A quiet month with a real fix beats four posts about nothing.

11What to build

Seven things. Build them once, use them for a year.

  • A 2.0 screenshot for the README. The Master tab with a few cards on and the Removed track selected. The old one showed 1.x, so the README has none right now.
  • Clip A, the noise alone. About 20 seconds. Track, then the Removed track alone. Your single most convincing asset.
  • A before and after pair. Same track, loudness matched, so people judge the sound and not the volume. Say that you matched them.
  • The verdict screenshot. The release check saying the file is ready to upload.
  • A five minute walkthrough. Install, drop a file, pick cards, listen to Removed, Clean & Master, download. Real time, including the wait. The getting-started guide is the script.
  • A first run answer. A short note about the Windows download warning and what to click, so it never becomes a bad first impression.
  • Three card examples. A fizzy track, a harsh vocal, a muddy mix, each with the card you picked and what the Removed track sounded like.

12How to tell if it is working

You cannot count users, and that is on purpose. Shimmer never phones home, so there are no install numbers, no usage reports, and no way to see who is running it. That is a promise worth more than the data. Measure what people do in public instead.

What to countWhereA good first month
Release downloadsThe GitHub releases pageAny number above zero. Watch the direction, not the size.
StarsGitHubRough interest over time. Do not chase it.
Issues and bug reportsGitHubThree. Reports mean real people ran it on real tracks.
Comments that ask a questionReddit, DiscordMore than the upvotes. A question means someone is deciding.
Watch timeShort video, YouTubeDid they stay until the Removed track played? That is the whole test.
People saying it out loudAnywhereOne person recommending Shimmer to someone else beats a hundred views.

Every month, write down the single best thing that happened and the single worst. Do more of the first. That is the whole review.

13What could go wrong

RiskWhat to do about it
A community treats you as a spammer Help for two weeks before you post anything of your own. Say you made it in the first line. If a post is removed, ask the moderators what would be allowed, and thank them.
The Shimmer card does less than people hope Say so first: it turns the fizz down, it is the newest fix, and you want to hear their results. Ask for a clip where it falls short. That is how the next version gets better.
Someone asks about clicks or grainy reverb tails Be plain: no fix yet, the de-click did not pass its tests, and it is on the list. Point to the changelog's Known issues.
The first Shimmer run feels stuck Say it before they hit it: about 50 seconds for a 3-minute song, once, with progress shown at the bottom.
People argue about AI music itself Do not take the bait. Shimmer fixes audio problems. Post where AI music is welcome and let the rest go.
The Windows download warning scares people off Say it before they hit it. Explain what to click and that the launcher is short enough to read in a text editor.
Someone tests a claim and it does not hold This is the expensive one. Keep to the checked numbers in the message reference, and never say a thing you have not seen the app do.
You get bored The most likely ending. Two hours a week beats a burst and a year of silence. Cut the plan down before you drop it.

14More post ideas

When the posts are used up, these are the next angles. All true, all ready to write.

  • Match a song you love. Load a reference track and the tone moves toward it, half the difference by default, never more than 3 dB.
  • The whistles nobody talks about. Thin steady tones high up that never move. Analyze finds them, and the notch filter took out 96 % of one in testing.
  • The release copy. 16-bit, 44.1 kHz, with dither: the file stores ask for, in WAV or FLAC.
  • See what happens to your audio. The Signal Chain tab shows every stage in order, with nothing hidden.
  • Tags that stay with the file, including a note of every fix that ran.
  • An EQ that suggests its own moves. Fix first, shape second, cuts before boosts, and nothing big.
  • What each Amount slider really does, and how to find the gentlest setting that works.
  • What is new in each release, once it ships: say what you fixed and who asked for it.
