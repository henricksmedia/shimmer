02

Name the problem

#
Reddit Discord Long posts
That thin, fizzy hiss on your AI track is not in your song. The model added it while it built the audio. It rides on top of the cymbals and gives vocals a glassy sheen. It mostly sits high up, between 5 and 12 kHz. Turn it up on good speakers and it gets worse. It is not your mix and it is not your performance. It is a generation artifact, and it can be taken out.
Why it works: it names a thing people hear but cannot describe. Post it with no link. Let someone ask what fixes it.
03

Clean first, master second

#
Reddit Short posts Comment reply
Master an AI track first and you just make the fizz louder. Normal mastering tools cannot tell that noise from your music. So they compress it and turn it up with everything else. Clean first. Master second. Shimmer does both, in that order.
Why it works: it is a rule people can use even if they never download the app. That is what makes it worth sharing.
04

Hear what you removed

#
Best with video YouTube Reddit
Most cleanup tools ask you to trust them. Shimmer lets you listen. Press 1 while your song plays and you hear the original. Press 2 for the cleaned version. Press 3 and you hear only the part that was removed, turned up loud. That third one is the safety check. It should sound like hiss and sizzle. If you hear vocals, a snare or a melody in there, you are cutting too hard. Turn the strength down.
Why it works: this is the feature no one else offers, and it answers the fear that a cleaner will eat the music. Pair it with a screen recording of the three keys.
05

Free, and it stays on your machine

#
Short posts Hacker news Reddit
Shimmer is free, and your music never leaves your computer. No account. No upload. No subscription. Nothing you make is used to train anything. Once it is set up you can turn the internet off and it still works. It is free on music you sell, too.
Why it works: most rivals are web apps with credits and an upload step. This is the clearest gap between you and them.
06

You do not have to know what to pick

#
Reddit Discord Beginner help threads
Worried you will pick the wrong setting? Do not pick one. Click Analyze. Shimmer listens to your whole song, then tries all 19 cleanup presets on the busiest few seconds. It measures what each one actually took out: noise, or your music. Then it shows you the best fit, tells you why in plain words, and sets the strength for you. It takes about ten seconds. If a second pass would help, it says so.
Why it works: "I do not know what any of this means" is the biggest thing stopping people. This removes it.
07

Loudness without squashing

#
Reddit Producers Long posts
Shimmer sets loudness with one steady gain move. No multiband compression. The dynamics you generated are the dynamics you keep. Pick a target and it does the rest. Streaming is −14 LUFS, Loud is −11, CD and club is −9. Spotify and Apple Music turn everything toward −14 anyway, so mastering louder than that just gets turned back down. A true-peak limiter holds the ceiling so your track will not clip once it becomes an MP3.
Why it works: it speaks to people who know the terms and are checking whether you do. Every number here is real.
08

One verdict before you upload

#
Best with a screenshot Short posts Reddit
The scary part of releasing a track is not knowing if the file is right. When Shimmer finishes, it checks the file it just made and gives you one answer: ready to upload, or here is what to look at. It checks loudness, true peak, clipping, sample rate, silence at the start and end, mono playback and your tags. Then it tells you how loud the track will actually play on Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube.
Why it works: it sells confidence, not features. Post a screenshot of the green verdict card next to it.
09

A whole album, kept in shape

#
Reddit Album makers Long posts
Mastering an album one track at a time flattens it. Every song gets pushed to the same level, so the quiet one hits as hard as the single. Point Shimmer at the folder and turn on Album mode. It cleans every track first. Then one gain move brings the loudest track to your target, and the rest keep their distance below it. Your quiet song stays quieter. That is what a record is supposed to do.
Why it works: it reaches people finishing a project, not just a single track. They are the ones who will use Shimmer every week.

Who made it, and why
