05

Tell it what you hear

#
Best with video Reddit Discord Beginner help threads
You do not need to know what a de-esser is. Shimmer 2.0 asks what you hear instead. Harsh "s" sounds. A fizzy hiss up top. Muddy, boxy low mids. A whistle that never changes. There are ten cards, each named for a sound. Pick the one that matches and it turns on the tool made for that problem, with one Amount slider. Not sure what you are hearing? Click Analyze. It finds steady whistles, and tells you how far your song is from release loudness.
Why it works: "I do not know what any of this means" is the biggest thing stopping people. The first line takes that fear away before it can form.
06

Hear what you removed

#
Best with video Short video YouTube Reddit
Most cleanup tools ask you to trust them. Shimmer lets you listen. Press 1 while your song plays and you hear the original. Press 2 for the cleaned version. Press 3 and you hear only what was removed, turned up. That third one is your safety check. It should sound like hiss and grit. If you hear vocals, a snare or a melody in there, that fix is cutting too hard. Turn its Amount down.
Why it works: it answers the fear that a cleaner will eat the music, and it teaches a habit people keep. Pair it with video script A below.
07

What you preview is what you export

#
Short posts Producers Reddit
The worst feeling in audio: it sounded right in the preview, and the export sounds different. In Shimmer 2.0 the preview and the export are the same render. The preview just plays a short part of it. A test runs on every update and fails if the two ever drift apart. So when it sounds right in the loop, it is right in the file.
Why it works: everyone who has exported a mix knows this feeling. It opens with the pain and closes with a promise the app keeps.
08

Loud like released songs

#
Reddit Producers Short video Long posts
Your AI track is not quiet. It is unmastered. Shimmer 2.0 masters to −9 LUFS by default, about as loud as most released songs. Want more room? Pick Balanced (−11) or Streaming standard (−14 LUFS). It gets there with one steady gain move for the whole song, then a peak shaper and a true-peak limiter. No multiband compression. And every MP3, OGG and M4A is decoded after it is made and checked, so it will not clip when it plays.
Why it works: the first line reframes a complaint people already have, and the rest speaks to anyone checking whether you know the terms. Every number here is real.
09

Every fix had to earn its place

#
Producers Reddit LinkedIn Dev spaces
How do you know a cleanup tool is not hurting your music? Before each fix in Shimmer 2.0 went in, I planted the problem it fixes in five real songs and measured two things: how much of the problem it took out, and how much it took from the same song with nothing wrong. The top of each Amount slider is set by that second number. The de-click did not meet the bar, so its card says "Not built yet" and changes nothing. Every change, with its numbers, is in the changelog.
Why it works: it earns trust by showing the test, and by admitting a fix that failed it. Skeptics respect that more than any feature list.

One verdict before you upload

#
Best with a screenshot Short posts Reddit
The scary part of releasing a track is not knowing if the file is right. When Shimmer finishes, it checks the file it just wrote and gives you one answer: ready to upload, or here is what to look at. It checks loudness, true peak, clipping in your source, sample rate, silence at the start and end, mono playback and your tags. Then it shows how loud the track will play on the big streaming apps.
Why it works: it sells confidence, not features. Post a screenshot of the release check next to it.
12

A whole album, kept in shape

#
Reddit Album makers Long posts
Mastering an album one track at a time flattens it. Every song gets pushed to the same level, so the quiet one hits as hard as the single. Point Shimmer's Batch tab at the folder and turn on Album mode. It measures every track first. Then one gain move brings the loudest track to your target, and the rest keep their distance below it. Your quiet song stays quieter. That is what a record is supposed to do.
Why it works: it reaches people finishing a project, not just a single track. They are the ones who will use Shimmer every week.
13

Split the song, keep the parts

#
Best with video YouTube Reddit
Want the vocal on its own? Or the song without it? Shimmer's Remix tab splits a song into 4 or 6 stems: vocals, drums, bass and the rest, plus guitar and piano on the 6-stem model. Mix them on faders, add formant, saturation, doubler or reverb to any lane, then export through the same cleanup and mastering as the Master tab. A Residual lane holds whatever the split missed, so the parts still add up to your song.
Why it works: "instrumental" and "acapella" are things people already search for. The Residual lane answers the producer who asks what the split threw away.

Name the fizz

#
Reddit Discord No link
That thin, fizzy hiss on your AI track is not in your song. The model added it while it built the audio. It rides on the cymbals and gives vocals a glassy sheen, mostly high up, between 5 and 12 kHz. Turn it up on good speakers and it gets worse. It is not your mix and it is not your performance. It is a generation artifact, and it can be turned down.
Why it works: it names a thing people hear but cannot describe. Post it with no link and let someone ask what helps.

First lines that stop the scroll

People decide in the first line of a post, or the first two seconds of a video. Each line below is true and can open any post above. Pick one, then say one thing and ask one question.

Name the pain

  • That fizzy hiss on your AI track is not in your song.
  • Your AI track is not quiet. It is unmastered.
  • Why does my AI song sound thin on real speakers?
  • It sounded right in the preview. Then I exported it.

Make them curious

  • Press 3. This is everything I removed from my track.

Go against the grain

  • Do not master your AI track yet. Clean it first.

  • Louder is not the problem. Unmastered is.

Start a conversation

Three short video scripts

For TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts. Vertical, 15 to 30 seconds, screen recording of the app plus big on-screen text. Tell people to turn the sound on in the first second, because the proof is audio. Add captions for anyone who will not.

A · "This is what I removed" (about 20 seconds)

  1. 0–2 s. Text: That hiss isn't in your song. Sound on. The track plays, fizzy top end.
  2. 2–8 s. Click the Shimmer card. Text: I told it what I hear.
  3. 8–15 s. Press 3. Only the Removed track plays: hiss, nothing else. Text: This is everything it took out.
  4. 15–20 s. Press 2, the cleaned track. Text: Free. Runs on your computer. Link in bio.

Caption: post 06 or post 01. Why it works: the payoff is the moment the noise plays alone. Get there fast.

B · "Tell it what you hear" (about 25 seconds)

  1. 0–2 s. Text: You don't need to know what a de-esser is.
  2. 2–10 s. Scroll the ten cards slowly. Stop on Sibilance: "Harsh, spitty s and sh". Click it.
  3. 10–18 s. Play a vocal line, then press 3: the "s" sounds alone. Text: Too much? Turn the Amount down. Drag the slider.
  4. 18–25 s. Press 2. Text: Say what you hear. Hear it fixed.

Caption: post 05.

C · "Quiet next to everything else?" (about 20 seconds)

  1. 0–2 s. Text: Your AI track isn't quiet. It's unmastered.
  2. 2–8 s. Show the loudness cards, Commercial selected.
  3. 8–15 s. Click Clean & Master, then show the release check saying the file is ready.
  4. 15–20 s. Text: As loud as released songs. Checked before you upload.

Caption: post 08. When you play before and after, match their loudness and say that you did, or people only hear "louder".

Replies to keep ready

The same questions come up every time. Answer in plain words, and never argue about whether AI music is real music.

  • "Is this AI?" The cleanup is signal processing. The Shimmer card uses one small trained model that runs on your own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • "Does it upload my music?" No. There is no account and no server. Your files never leave your computer.
  • "Is it really free?" Yes, including for music you sell. The code is public to read, under the Shimmer License; it is not open source.
  • "Does it work on Mac?" Yes, on macOS and Linux as well as Windows. Windows is the most tested.
  • "Does it get rid of the Suno shimmer?" It turns the fizzy hiss down, and you can hear exactly what it took. It is the newest fix, so tell me what it does on your tracks.
  • "Can it fix clicks?" Not yet. The de-click did not pass my tests, so it is not switched on. It is on the list.
  • "Why is the Shimmer card slow the first time?" It reads the whole song once, about 50 seconds for a 3-minute song. After that it is quick.
  • "Why not an online mastering service?" They master the noise along with the music, and your file goes to their server. Shimmer cleans first and never sees your file.
