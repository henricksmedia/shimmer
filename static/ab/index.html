      Keys, and what "Difference" can and cannot tell you
      

        
space play ·  · S next version ·
           D difference · 
           L loop four seconds from here ·
           0 back to loop start ·
           ← → previous / next comparison

        
During a listening check:
           X hear the unknown one, the version's own number to hear
           it, and ⇧ with that number to answer.

        
Difference plays
        what one version has and the other does not. It tells you whether the
        thing taken out was noise or music. It cannot tell you whether
        too much was taken: removed air sounds like shhh on its own and
        never like music, so a track can lose real openness while the removed
        part still sounds like nothing. Judge how much by swapping, not by
        listening to the difference.
