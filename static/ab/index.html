Keys, and what "Difference" can and cannot tell you

space play · S swap · D difference · L loop the last 4 s · 0 restart · ← → previous / next comparison

Difference plays what one version has and the other does not. It tells you whether the thing taken out was noise or music. It cannot tell you whether too much was taken: removed air sounds like shhh on its own and never like music, so a track can lose real openness while the removed part still sounds like nothing. Judge how much by swapping, not by listening to the difference.
